Part of #1905. Depends on #2196.

**Goal:** a learner in a unit on a frontend-base site has the content tools as on `master`: on a course with the calculator enabled, the calculator bar below the unit expands, evaluates an equation through the LMS and shows the math help link; on a course with notes enabled, the notes toggle flips the learner's notes visibility through the LMS and tells the unit to show or hide its notes. Neither renders on a course that does not enable it. The content-tools stub from #2196 is replaced.

## What moves

Files port from the legacy `src/` tree to the same relative path under `frontend-base/src/`, with their tests, and get a line each in `frontend-base/manifest.tsv`.

- The content tools ([`src/courseware/course/content-tools/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/tree/master/src/courseware/course/content-tools)): the container, which the course component renders below the sequence and which shows each tool on its course flag (`showCalculator`, `notes.enabled` from the courseware metadata); the calculator, a collapsible that sends the equation to the LMS `calculate` endpoint and links to the math help page; the notes toggle, which puts the new visibility to the LMS edxnotes endpoint and then posts `tools.toggleNotes` to the unit iframe; their styles and messages.
- Two config reads change form, per the reviewed config table: `LMS_BASE_URL` becomes the site config's `lmsBaseUrl`; `SUPPORT_URL_CALCULATOR_MATH` becomes a key of Learning's app config under the same name, read through `getAppConfig`, the form the frontend-base how-to gives for app-specific settings.

## Testing

The tests of the files that move here port with them: the container's flag-driven rendering, the calculator's expand, instructions and equation submission against a mocked `calculate` request, and the notes toggle's two states and its click against a mocked PUT, with the unit iframe's message asserted. They reach the page through the exported route array at a unit URL, with the courseware metadata mocked per the conventions #2189 set.

## Acceptance

- [ ] On a course with the calculator enabled, the calculator expands, evaluates an equation and collapses; its help link points at the app-config URL; on a course without it, no calculator renders
- [ ] On a course with notes enabled, the notes toggle flips, the LMS visibility endpoint receives the new value, and the unit iframe receives the toggle message; on a course without notes, no toggle renders
- [ ] Types, lint and tests of the new package pass; the legacy workflow stays green; `npm run dev` from the package serves a unit with both tools on a course that enables them
- [ ] Manifest lines added for every file moved; the content-tools directory's coverage compared against the `master` baseline
