## Facts

- The course bar's tabs are `CourseTabsNavigation` in [`shell/header/course-bar/navigation/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-base/tree/ae5ec935e53e084ba6f3be0521ecb1cec274810f/shell/header/course-bar/navigation) (added for #149 in #106, moved under `course-bar/` in #260). It renders a Paragon `Nav variant="tabs"` in a `Navbar` with the class `course-tabs-navigation`, and [`course-tabs-navigation.scss`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-base/blob/ae5ec935e53e084ba6f3be0521ecb1cec274810f/shell/header/course-bar/navigation/course-tabs-navigation.scss) sets two rules: a 2px bottom border on the bar in `--pgn-color-nav-tabs-base-border-base`, and no bottom border on `.nav-tabs`. Everything else is Paragon's tabs styling.
- On Learning's `master`, the tabs are Learning's own [`CourseTabsNavigation`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/230799eb258f0434a56a87c88e18c920e30f67f3/src/course-tabs/CourseTabsNavigation.tsx#L26-L31), a `Tabs` list with the class `nav-underline-tabs`, styled by [`src/index.scss`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/230799eb258f0434a56a87c88e18c920e30f67f3/src/index.scss#L40-L61):
  - each `.nav-link` has 4px transparent top and bottom borders, no side borders or radius, and `--pgn-color-gray-700` text;
  - on hover, focus and `.active`, the text and the bottom border turn `--pgn-color-primary-500`, at normal font weight.
- Learning's frontend-base package mounts the shell's course bar, so on the ported dates tab (openedx/frontend-app-learning#2234) the tabs take the course bar's look, not `master`'s.
