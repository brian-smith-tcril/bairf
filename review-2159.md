### 9. `CoursePathwaysStrip.scss`: what Paragon already provides, what it does not

Status: open: Brian flagged the file on his read (2026-11-09): custom styles are a cost to theme authors; comment shape pending
Rests on: src/pathways/pathways-strip/CoursePathwaysStrip.scss (53 lines), CoursePathwaysStrip.tsx, MorePathwaysPopover.tsx at 696901a030; @openedx/paragon 23.23.1 dist/core.css (installed; package.json `^23.4.5`); `git ls-files 'src/**/*.scss'`

Rule by rule against Paragon 23.23.1's `core.css`:

- `.course-pathways-strip-container` (`position: relative; flex: 1; min-width: 0; overflow: hidden`): `position-relative`, `flex-grow-1`, `overflow-hidden` exist as utilities; no utility sets `min-width: 0` (`mw-100` is max-width), and that line is what lets the label row shrink under its content. One line needs SCSS.
- `.course-pathways-strip-label` (`flex-shrink: 0; white-space: nowrap`): `flex-shrink-0` and `text-nowrap` exist. The `& + .label` separator (0.75rem margin and padding, 1px `--pgn-color-light-700` border, logical `inline-start`): Paragon's spacer scale is 1..6 = .25/.5/1/1.5/3/5rem, no .75; `border-left` exists but is `--pgn-color-border` with `!important` and there is no `border-light-700` colour utility; so the separator is SCSS either way. Design question, not a Paragon one: is `light-700` the divider colour the Figma wants on `bg-primary`?
- `.course-pathways-strip-trigger` on the Paragon `Button`: `py-0` and `rounded-0` exist as utilities (Paragon utilities are `!important`, so they beat `.btn-sm`); the `border-inline-start` is the same separator as above. Then it reaches into Paragon component internals: `.badge` is rebuilt (`inline-flex`, `min-width`/`height` 1.25rem, `padding: 0`, margin) against Paragon's own badge tokens (`--pgn-spacing-badge-padding-*`, `--pgn-size-badge-border-radius-pill`), and `.btn-icon-after`'s 0.5rem gap is zeroed. No other SCSS file in this repo targets `.badge` or `.btn-icon-after` (`git grep` over `src/**/*.scss`, none). This block is the "custom where the system provides" item: the system's `Badge pill` and the button's icon spacing are provided and then overridden.
- `.course-pathways-strip-measure` (hidden copy for measuring): `position-absolute` and `invisible` exist; `top/left: 0`, `width: max-content`, `pointer-events: none` have no Paragon 23 utility (`top-0`, `pe-none` are Bootstrap 5). Stays SCSS, and the measuring approach itself is entry 6's "+N" reserve question.

Why it matters (Brian, 2026-11-09): every custom rule is one more thing a theme author has to find and override; a repo already carrying custom SCSS is no licence to add more. The file's size relative to siblings is not a consideration. The ask is therefore the whole file, not the Badge block alone: utilities where Paragon has them, and for the lines Paragon cannot express (`min-width: 0`, the separator, the measuring block) the question is whether the design needs them at all.

### 16. The PR's "+N" trigger looks broken on hover and when opened

Status: open: a problem with the PR as it stands (Brian, 2026-11-24, from his local run at 3201351b93 with the dummy-data patch); candidate comment, not drafted
Rests on: Brian's three screenshots, saved as `images/trigger-pr-rest.png`, `images/trigger-pr-hover.png`, `images/trigger-pr-open.png` (staff user, wide viewport, three labels visible, two in the popover); src/pathways/pathways-strip/{MorePathwaysPopover.tsx, CoursePathwaysStrip.scss} at 3201351b93; @openedx/paragon 23.23.1 dist/core.min.css (`.btn`, `.btn-inverse-tertiary`, the `:focus::before` ring) and dist/light.min.css (the inverse-tertiary tokens)

What Brian saw, his words "looks bad" for both states:

- At rest: the separator line, the light circle with the count, the triangle. As designed.
- On hover: a lighter rectangle with square corners appears behind the circle and triangle, and the separator line before the trigger disappears.
- Clicked: a white outline shows only as a bracket on each side of the trigger, `[ 2 ▾ ]`, with no top or bottom edge, the left one tight against the last label's final letter. The popover opens below, right-aligned to the trigger, over the instructor toolbar: a white box with a bulleted list of the two hidden pathway names in grey body text.

Why, read from the code (not confirmed in the browser's inspector):

- The separator is the button's own left border (`border-inline-start: 1px solid var(--pgn-color-light-700)` on `.course-pathways-strip-trigger`). Paragon's `.btn` draws its border from `--pgn-btn-border-color` and swaps it on hover for the variant's hover border colour, which for `inverse-tertiary` is fully transparent (`#00000000`), so the line goes when the pointer arrives. The rectangle is the variant's hover background (`#FFFFFF1A`, white at 10%), square because the SCSS sets `border-radius: 0`.
- The focus ring is Paragon's `:focus::before` box, drawn outside the button on all four sides (2px wide, white for this variant, offset by the focus gap, with a radius). The SCSS takes the button's vertical padding to zero, so the button is as tall as the row, and the trigger sits inside `.course-pathways-strip-container`, which is `overflow: hidden`: the ring's top and bottom fall outside that box and are clipped, leaving the two sides. A clipped focus indicator is an accessibility matter as well as a visual one (Claude's reading).
- The popover is `ModalPopup` around a hand-built `div` (`bg-white px-4 py-3 rounded shadow small`) and `ul`; the Figma's has an arrow pointing at the trigger and its items are underlined links (entry 15), which the items are not until #2231.

All three follow from the custom rules on the trigger and the container, the ones entry 9 is about: each override of the Paragon `Button` (radius, padding, a border reused as a separator) changes a state the author did not look at. That makes this the concrete case for entry 9's ask (Claude's reading). The 2026-11-24 trial (entry 15) did not reach a replacement for the trigger.

### 17. Three ways to build the strip with no stylesheet of its own

Chrome will not open a window under 500px, so narrow widths need the emulation the script uses; `--window-size=400` silently gives a 500px page cropped.

Common to all three, each seen on the test page:

- The lead-in sits inside the `nav` and is what the `nav` is `aria-labelledby`. One string, read once.

- A separator is `border-left border-light-700` on a wrapper around the link or the `Dropdown`, never on the `Button`, so no hover state can erase it.

Option 1, one line: what fits shows, the rest goes in the menu. Paragon's `useIndexOfLastVisibleChild` does the measuring, as in Paragon's `Tabs` and this repo's course tabs. Every pathway renders once; the ones past the index get `position-absolute invisible mw-100 overflow-hidden`, out of the row but still measurable. Gone from the PR: `hooks.ts` and its 86px constant, `hooks.test.tsx`, the measure row, `CoursePathwaysStrip.scss`, and `MorePathwaysPopover`'s `ModalPopup` and hand-built box. Two things it needs that are not obvious: Paragon's `Nav` does not forward a ref, so the element is reached as the `firstElementChild` of a wrapper that has one (Paragon's `Tabs` does the same); and the lead-in is inside the `nav` so that the `nav` is a block whose width comes from `container-xl`. The hook's own limits stay: it always reserves the trigger's width, and it re-measures on a container resize only. Closest to the Figma. Where it differs: the count is `Badge pill` (an oval), the caret is the toggle's chevron, and the opened state is a menu, not a box with an arrow. No measuring, no hidden pathways, no menu, nothing to clip; about twenty lines. One message carries the sentence with the lead-in as a rich-text chunk, so a translation can reorder it. Its cost is height: with the five fixtures, one of them 76 characters, two lines at 1440px, three at 800px, five at 390px; one line for one or two pathways down to tablet width.

Option 3, the sentence with a cap: the first two pathways are named and the rest are behind an inline "N more" `Dropdown`, used only when two or more would be left over. Height stays bounded (two lines at 390px with the fixtures). It hides names on a wide screen where they would have fit.
