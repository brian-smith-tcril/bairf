# frontend-app-learning glossary

Terms specific to this repo. Ecosystem terms are in `../../GLOSSARY.md`.

- **courseware**: the unit-viewing side of the app (`src/courseware/`), as opposed to **course home** (`src/course-home/`, the outline, dates, progress and discussion tabs).
- **model store**: the former Redux-backed normalized cache read through `useModel`, dissolved in #1977.
- **running stack**: see the ecosystem glossary; here, every layer of epic #1946 landed on one.
