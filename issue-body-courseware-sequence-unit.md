Part of #1905. Depends on #2195.

**Goal:** a learner opens a unit on a frontend-base site and sees its content, moves with previous and next across units and sequence boundaries, sees the hidden-after-due notice on a past-due sequence, the course license line and the sequence alerts, as on `master`. The course stub from #2195 is replaced by the course component. What the course component renders beyond that, the sidebars, exams, content tools and celebrations, is stubbed here and replaced by its own issue.

**What a learner does not see, by design:** no navigation sidebar yet (#2197), no breadcrumbs and no unit tab strip. The last two are operator plugins on `master` with no default content ([`CourseBreadcrumbsSlot`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/plugin-slots/CourseBreadcrumbsSlot/index.tsx), [`SequenceNavigationSlot`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/plugin-slots/SequenceNavigationSlot/index.jsx)), so a stock `master` deployment shows neither either; previous and next render outside the slot and remain.

## What moves

Files port from the legacy `src/` tree to the same relative path under `frontend-base/src/`, with their tests, and get a line each in `frontend-base/manifest.tsv`.

- The course component ([`Course.jsx`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/courseware/course/Course.jsx)) with its page title, and the sequence ([`Sequence.jsx`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/courseware/course/sequence/Sequence.jsx)): sequence content, the unit with its content iframe, suspense and URL building, unit navigation (previous and next, with the course-exit mode helper and messages the next button reads for its end-of-course text and enabling, [`course-exit/utils.js`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/courseware/course/course-exit/utils.js), which port here ahead of the course exit page), hidden-after-due, the course license line, and the sequence alerts (banner text, entrance exam). The sequence's `<main>` landmark around its content (#2026) ports without its `id="main-content"`, which the shell's content wrapper carries; the shell's skip link finds the `<main>` element itself (openedx/frontend-base#329).
- The unit computes its iframe URL with `getIFrameUrl` directly. On `master` it folds the result through Learning's override registry ([`plugin-overrides`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/blob/master/src/generic/plugin-overrides/PluginOverridesContext.tsx)), which the port drops (#2189 gives the module `dropped` manifest lines): on a frontend-base site no plugin can reach the registry, since the hook is not a package export and, until #2185, there is no slot to mount a widget in. #2223 reshapes the override onto the Slot API. The URL is the default one, as on a stock `master` deployment.
- Plugin slots rendered by these components become placeholder slots (file and name kept, default content in a bare fragment, no plugin-framework import): content-iframe loader (default content: the page loader), unit title (default content: the heading, the unit navigation and the bookmark button; the button's component and data module under [`bookmark/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/tree/master/src/courseware/course/bookmark) port with it, the mutation creating or deleting the bookmark through the LMS and updating the unit in the sequence-metadata query), sequence container, sequence navigation, sequence bottom navigation, the submitted-timed-exam instructions slot, the course breadcrumbs slot, and the learner tools slot (the empty slot that replaced the Xpert chat trigger, rendered beside the breadcrumbs on wide viewports). A slot with no default content (breadcrumbs, learner tools, sequence container, sequence navigation) is a module rendering an empty fragment.

## Stubbed here, replaced by later issues

The course and sequence components import these directly on `master`. Each is a stub in this issue that renders nothing or passes children through, and each is deleted by the issue that ports the real thing:

| Content tools | the content tools issue |
| First-section and weekly-goal celebration modals | the celebrations issue |

## Testing

The sequence, unit and navigation tests port with their files and reach the page through the exported route array at a unit URL, with the courseware, sequence and metadata queries mocked per the conventions #2189 set. Tests of stubbed pieces port with those pieces, not here.
