The plan block of the issue body, mirrored; the effort decision numbers are kept here and restated in words there. Written 2026-11-10, posted 2026-11-10.

## Baseline

- The package at the top of #2234: the tab-page frame with the root providers, the query defaults and the error-logging listener, the course-home data module with the progress pieces already in it (`progressTabDataQuery`, `useProgressTabData`, `examAttemptsDataQuery`, `useExamAttemptsData`, `useRequestCert`, `getProgressTabData`, `getExamsData`, with their `api.test.js` and `apiHooks.test.tsx` cases and the `progressTabData` factory), both progress paths as stub routes, the five stubs for the toolbar, tours and streak modal, the placeholder-slot pattern, the test conventions of decision 23, the global 96.15 line threshold.
- On `master`, the progress tab's import closure (walked from `ProgressTab.jsx`, with the frame, the data module and the stubbed pieces left out) is the 40 files of `course-home/progress-tab/` (37 source and message files, two tests, a README), the seven progress slot modules, `shared/links.jsx`, `courseware/course/course-exit/utils.js` with its `messages.ts` and `utils.test.ts`, and `course-tabs/hooks.ts` with its test, which #2189 dropped with the tabs navigation and whose only other readers are here. 53 files move. The closure also reads `useCoursewareMetadata` from `courseware/data/apiHooks`, with `enabled: false`, which is stubbed (below).
- frontend-platform symbols the closure uses, all exported from `@openedx/frontend-base`'s root: `useIntl`, `FormattedMessage`, `FormattedDate`, `defineMessages`, `getLocale`, `isRtl`, `getAuthenticatedUser`, `sendTrackEvent`, `camelCaseObject` (tests); `getConfig` becomes `getSiteConfig` or `getAppConfig(appId)` per key; `AppProvider` appears only in the test and goes with the render helper.
- Config keys the closure reads: `LMS_BASE_URL` (the 404 redirect in `ProgressTab.jsx`, the certificate web view URL in `CertificateStatus.jsx`) is `getSiteConfig().lmsBaseUrl`; `CREDIT_HELP_LINK_URL` (`CreditInformation.jsx`) and `SUPPORT_URL_ID_VERIFICATION` (`links.jsx`) are app config keys under their names with a bundled default of `null`, as `master` defaults them; `SHOW_UNGRADED_ASSIGNMENT_PROGRESS` (`utils.ts`) is an app config key with a bundled default of `false`, and `showUngradedAssignments` keeps both its `'true'` and `true` checks; `ACCOUNT_PROFILE_URL` (`links.jsx`) is replaced by the profile role (decision 34).

## What moves

- `plugin-slots/ProgressCertificateStatusSlot`, `ProgressTabCertificateStatusMainBodySlot`, `ProgressTabCertificateStatusSidePanelSlot`, `ProgressTabCourseCompletionSlot`, `ProgressTabCourseGradeSlot`, `ProgressTabGradeBreakdownSlot`, `ProgressTabRelatedLinksSlot`: the seven index files, as placeholder slots. Their READMEs and images (7 and 9 files) are dropped with `dropped` manifest lines, as #2189 did for the banner slot's: they document the plugin-framework contract, and #2185 writes the Slot API ones.
- `shared/links.jsx`, with a new `links.test.tsx` beside it (no test on `master`; its lines were covered through the progress and course-exit suites).
- `courseware/course/course-exit/utils.js`, `messages.ts` and `utils.test.ts`. The certificate status reads the mode helper (`COURSE_EXIT_MODES`, `getCourseExitMode`) and the links module reads three of the messages, so this is their first consumer in page order; decision 29's amendment had them port with CW2's unit navigation (#2196) and is amended by this plan. Both files port whole: `GetCourseExitNavigation`, `logClick` and `logVisit` ride along without a caller until #2196 and #2202, and `utils.js`'s two courseware hooks come from the stub below.
- `course-tabs/hooks.ts` and `hooks.test.tsx`: their `dropped` manifest lines become port lines. `RelatedLinks.jsx` reads `useCourseOutlineUrl` and `useDatesTabUrl`, `DetailedGrades.jsx` reads `useCourseOutlineUrl`. On `master` the hooks return the tab URLs the LMS sends in the course metadata (`tabs[].url`: for the dates and courseware tabs, absolute Learning URLs the LMS builds from its learning URL setting, `lms/djangoapps/courseware/tabs.py` in openedx/edx-platform), rendered as anchors, so each click is a full page load. Here they resolve Learning's own roles (effort decision 38): `useDatesTabUrl` returns `resolveRouteByRole(courseDatesRole, { courseId }).url` when the `tabs` list has a dates tab and `undefined` otherwise, `useCourseOutlineUrl` the same with `courseOutlineRole`, `useProgressTabUrl` with `courseProgressRole`; `tabs` gates presence only. Since the metadata's URL is never read, the three URL getters in `course-tabs/utils.ts` that only these hooks call (`getCourseOutlineUrl`, `getDatesTabUrl`, `getProgressTabUrl`) become presence checks beside `hasDiscussionTab` (`hasCourseOutlineTab`, `hasDatesTab`, `hasProgressTab`), with their cases in `utils.test.ts`, and each hook reads as its two facts: the role's URL, returned when the course has the tab. The three readers render `Hyperlink` with `getLinkProps(url)`, so a path in this site is a react-router `Link` and the root providers stay mounted across the switch. The hooks test keeps its two cases and gains the absent-tab case; the first now expects a URL that a route carrying the tab's role matches for the course, read from the exported route array as `courseware/urls.test.ts` does. Known gap, stated in the PR body: a site cannot point one of these roles elsewhere, because `getUrlByRouteRole` returns an installed app's route for a role before it reads the site's `externalRoutes`, and Learning declares the three roles for the course bar; the override works once openedx/frontend-base#348 is resolved.
- `courseware/urls.ts`, new, with `urls.test.ts`: `getSequenceUrl(courseId, sequenceId)`, the URL of a sequence's courseware page. `coursewareRole` is declared on three routes (the course's index route, `:sequenceId`, `:sequenceId/:unitId`) and `getUrlByRouteRole` returns the first route that carries a role, so the role resolves to the course's courseware path and `resolveRouteByRole` has no path to fill a `sequenceId` into. The function resolves the role for the course and appends the sequence id; it throws when no route provides the role, which cannot happen while Learning's own routes are installed. That rests on one assumption, that the role's URL is the base of `/:sequenceId`: the test holds Learning's own routes to it by matching the returned URL against the exported route array. `master` writes `/course/${courseId}/${sequenceId}` and its unit form inline (the outline tab's `SequenceTitle.tsx`, `JumpNavMenuItem.jsx`, `BreadcrumbItem.tsx`, `ContentLock.jsx`, the sequence navigation's `hooks.js` and `UnitButton.tsx`, the navigation sidebar's `UnitLinkWrapper.tsx`); each of those calls this function, or a unit form added beside it in this file, when its page ports. The progress subsections are its first caller.

- The courseware metadata slice of `courseware/data/`: `coursewareMeta.ts` whole (the normalizer and the `CoursewareMeta` type; it imports `getTimeOffsetMillis` from the ported course-home API); `getCourseMetadata` from `api.js` and `coursewareMetadataQuery` with `useCoursewareMetadata` from `apiHooks.ts`, each file with one manifest line, #2195 adding the rest of the file to the same target; the `courseMetadata` factory (it extends the ported `courseMetadataBase`) with a `__factories__/index.js` whose other four imports are commented out under #2195's URL, as the course-home index does for #2191. `master` has no test of the metadata query or the normalizer (`apiHooks.test.tsx` covers sequence metadata only), so the port adds cases beside them: the request URL with the browser timezone, the normalized shape, the hook's fetch. Reason: the entrance exam fix below makes the progress tab fetch this query (decision 39).

## What changes in the moved code

- Imports: every `@edx/frontend-platform*` import becomes one from `@openedx/frontend-base`; `getConfig()` reads become the site-config field or `getAppConfig(appId)` per the key table above. The 404 redirect to the LMS's legacy progress page keeps `global.location.replace`: it leaves the site.
- **Subsection links resolve the courseware role (decision 38).** On `master` each subsection title in `SubsectionTitleCell.jsx` is an `<a href>` to the LMS `jump_to` URL the progress response carries (`lms/djangoapps/course_home_api/progress/serializers.py`), which the LMS resolves to `{learning URL}/course/<courseId>/<subsectionKey>` (`get_courseware_url` in `openedx/features/course_experience/url_helpers.py`: a subsection is the sequence, no unit). Here the anchor's destination is `getSequenceUrl(courseId, blockKey)` (What moves) through `getLinkProps`, the same page reached client-side; the response's `url` stays the gate, since the LMS nulls it for a subsection a non-staff learner may no longer open, and the greyed span renders then as on `master`. The click handler and its tracking event are untouched. Until #2195 the link lands on the courseware stub, as the outline link lands on the outline stub until #2191. The studio button keeps the CMS URL from the response; Studio is not a Learning page.
- **The progress tab fetches the courseware metadata and gates on it (#2243, #2244; decision 39).** On `master` the certificate status reads `useCoursewareMetadata(courseId, { enabled: false })` for the entrance exam result and never has it: nothing on the progress tab fetches that query and every path into the tab is a full page load. Under the shell a tab switch is client-side, so once #2195 fills the cache from the courseware page the card would differ between a hard load and a switch. The fix from #2243 lands here: `ProgressTab` runs `useCoursewareMetadata(courseId)` as a fetching query when the route has no `targetUserId` and passes `tabDataQueries: [tabDataQuery, coursewareMetadataQuery]` to `TabWithTimer`, the array form `TabPage` already takes, so the card mounts with the status known, renders once, and its `visited` event carries the final state. With a `targetUserId` the query is neither run nor gated (#2244: the endpoint answers for the requester, and course staff cannot override that), and `CertificateStatus` does not use its `enabled: false` read of that query either: the query client is the site's and the key names no user, so the requester's own progress page, or a courseware page once #2195 lands, can leave the requester's status in the cache. The card passes `null` there whatever the cache holds, which is what the `enabled: false` read gives on a fresh load of that page, on `master` and here. Both conditions carry #2244's URL, the issue whose backend change removes them. The PR body states the deviation from `master` and why; #2243 and #2244 get a comment saying the fix is on `frontend-base`, linking the PR.
- **Links resolve roles (decision 34).** `DashboardLink` resolves `org.openedx.frontend.role.dashboard` with `getUrlByRouteRole`; `ProfileLink` resolves `org.openedx.frontend.role.profile` and appends `/u/<username>`, as the shell's `ProfileLinkMenuItem` does (a role that took the username as a param would replace the append, which waits on openedx/frontend-base#349, and a comment on the line says so); both render the Paragon `Hyperlink` with `getLinkProps(url)`, so a path in this site navigates client-side and an external route is a plain anchor. When the site declares no route for the role, the link renders nothing, as the shell does for both roles (`ProfileLinkMenuItem` returns `null`; the dev home page omits its dashboard link); a site that wants the LMS destination declares it as an external route. The two role strings go in `constants.ts` beside Learning's own. `IdVerificationSupportLink` reads `SUPPORT_URL_ID_VERIFICATION` from the app config and renders nothing when it is `null`, as on `master`.
- Placeholder slots: each of the seven keeps its file and component name and renders its default content in a bare fragment with no plugin-framework import. `ProgressCertificateStatusSlot` still renders its children; `ProgressTabCourseCompletionSlot` keeps its `enableProgressGraph` prop, which gates the chart as well as being a plugin prop; the main-body and side-panel slots keep their window-width gates. The props the slots passed to plugins (`courseId`, `enableProgressGraph`) are recorded in the manifest line's commit for #2185.
- Routes: both progress route objects (`progress`, `progress/:targetUserId`) become `lazy`, loading `./course-home/progress-tab/ProgressTab` (the directory has no index on `master`) with the `learning-progress` chunk name; the id stays on the first. Their page-rule assertion moves from `routes.test.tsx` to the progress test.
- Styles: `style.scss` gains `@use` lines for `CompletionDonutChart.scss` and `GradeBar.scss` (decision 33's pattern; both use only Paragon variables and their own). It also gains the three rules of `master`'s `src/index.scss` that style this tab and sit in no partial, copied as they are: `.greyed-out` (a subsection title without a link), `.locked-overlay` with its `.grades` variant (the grade breakdown of a fully locked course) and `.raised-card` (the completion, certificate, grade and breakdown cards); `src/index.scss` gets a manifest line pointing at `style.scss`.
- Console: the package's suite fails on any console output, so each warning the progress components raise is fixed in the component. `GradeSummaryHeader.jsx` and `GradeSummaryTableFooter.jsx` take `trigger={['hover', 'focus']}` in place of `trigger="hover"` (react-bootstrap's accessibility warning, 134 blocks on `master`, assigned to this port by the inventory). `AssignmentTypeCell`, `CurrentGradeTooltip`, `GradeRangeTooltip` and `PassingGradeTooltip` take default parameters in place of `defaultProps` on a function component, as the dates tab's components did in #2189. `GradeSummaryTable` set its parent's `allOfSomeAssignmentTypeIsLocked` state while rendering (React's "Cannot update a component while rendering a different component", assigned to this port by the inventory); it now derives the value while rendering and sets the parent's state in an effect. `NoticeRow`'s `message` prop type becomes `node`, since `CourseGradeFooter` passes it an element; `GradeRangeTooltip`'s `Popover` gets the `id` its prop types require. Paragon's own `defaultProps` warnings from components this page mounts (`Collapsible`'s parts, `DataTable`'s parts, `PopoverContent`) are silenced in the setup file by exact pattern under openedx/paragon#3043 and openedx/paragon#4523. The one `forwardRef render functions do not support propTypes` block the inventory attributes to the progress suite is not raised by the cases that run here, so nothing changes for it.
- Lint: `credit-information/messages.ts`'s `minimumGrade` and eight messages of `course-exit/messages.ts` gain the `description` the package's lint requires, with ids and default messages untouched; `getCourseExitMode`'s two `indexOf` comparisons become `includes`; `LockedDonutSegment` drops a `viewBox` attribute from a `<g>`, where it is not a valid attribute (`react/no-unknown-property`); `eslint-disable` comments for rules the package's lint does not have, or no longer reports, go; the autofixer's whitespace.
- The progress README ports; its plugin-framework example is replaced by one line under #2185's URL, since the Slot API port writes the widget example.
- `useExamsData` is not exported from the package index. `index.ts` exports the app and the routes, as account, profile and learner dashboard do (catalog adds its slot prop types); the hook is for widgets a site mounts in the progress slots, which is #2185's mechanism, so the export is decided there. The PR states this.

## Test changes

- `ProgressTab.test.jsx` reaches the page through the exported route array with `renderRoutes` at `/learning/course/<id>/progress` and `/learning/course/<id>/progress/10`; `fetchAndRender` wraps it and waits for the page's level-one heading before the loaded wait, since a lazy route has nothing fetching until it loads (#2189's dates test); the suite also imports the tab's module in a `beforeAll`, since the lazy route's first load can outlast `findBy`'s timeout. Track events are asserted on `getAnalyticsService().sendTrackEvent`. The `jest.mock` of `courseware-search/hooks` goes: its only readers were the tabs navigation and its slot, dropped in #2189. `logUnhandledRequests` goes: it answered every unmocked request with an empty 200 and a log line; under the adapter an unmocked request rejects, and the case that needed it fails, which is the better signal. The two `SHOW_UNGRADED_ASSIGNMENT_PROGRESS` cases use `mergeAppConfig(appId, …)` and restore the value after. The 404 case stubs `location.replace` with `mockLocation`.
- The five masquerade-banner cases and the "with the instructor toolbar mounted" request-count case render the toolbar for real and are kept whole as `it.skip` under #2194's URL (decision 37). The two "exam attempt data for plugins" cases mount a plugin through the plugin framework and cannot compile without it; they go in a block comment under #2185's URL, as `TabWithTimer.test.tsx`'s timer cases did. `hooks.test.jsx` ports whole at its component-router seam and keeps `useExamsData` covered.
- `links.test.tsx`, new: each link with its role declared as an app route and as an external route, the profile link's username suffix, nothing rendered when the role is undeclared, the support link present with the app config key set and absent when `null`.
- `courseware/urls.test.ts`, new: the returned URL matches a route of the exported array that carries `coursewareRole`, with the course and sequence ids as the route's params, and the function throws when the site config has no app. The function is written for Learning's own routes and nothing is asserted for the role as an external route: a site cannot point the role at one while Learning is installed (openedx/frontend-base#348), and an external route ignores the course id in any case (openedx/frontend-base#349).
- `course-exit/utils.test.ts` grows cases for `logClick` and `logVisit` (event names and the falsy guards). `GetCourseExitNavigation` gets none here: `master` tests it only through the sequence and unit navigation suites, which port with #2196, and it cannot be called without courseware metadata, since its first statement reads `entranceExamPassed` out of the metadata's `entranceExamData`. `course-tabs/hooks.test.tsx` ports as it is.
- The inline subsection fixtures of `master`'s progress test and the factory's second subsection gain the fields the components' prop types require (`block_key`, `problem_scores`, `learner_has_access`), since a prop-type failure is console output. A click on an anchor that leaves the site goes through `preventNavigation`, new in `tests/location.ts`: jsdom cannot follow a link and reports the attempt on the console.
- Cases added for lines no `master` test covers: in the progress suite, the header's studio link, the completion chart's segments and popovers, the related links and subsection titles (the role hrefs, no dates tab, a withheld `url`, practice scores), the credit requirements, the grade summary's percentages, the grade bar, further certificate states and a right-to-left locale; `utils.test.ts`, new, for `showUngradedAssignments` and `getLatestDueDateInFuture`; `ProgressTabComponents.test.tsx`, new, rendering one component alone: the complete segment's dividers, the four default parameters no caller leaves unset, and `CertificateStatus` without course metadata, whose `?? {}` changes the outcome (no card, a `certificate_status_disabled` event) and so keeps its line and gets the case.
- The progress suite gains the entrance exam cases: the gated learner sees no certificate card and the `visited` event is sent once, its `certificate_status_variant` being the progress response's `cert_status` unchanged, since the card has no variant for a failed entrance exam, here or on `master`; the ungated learner sees the card; the courseware metadata is requested once per load on the learner's own page and not on `progress/:targetUserId`; on `progress/:targetUserId` with the requester's failing status already in the query cache, the card renders as it does with the cache empty.
- The progress test asserts `expect(runPageRules(document)).toEqual([])` once the page has rendered.
