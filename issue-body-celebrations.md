## What moves

Files port from the legacy `src/` tree to the same relative path under `frontend-base/src/`, with their tests, and get a line each in `frontend-base/manifest.tsv`.

- The courseware celebrations ([`src/courseware/course/celebration/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/tree/master/src/courseware/course/celebration)): the first-section and weekly-goal modals, their helper module and LMS call. The helper module is also where the container's next-section handler writes the marker the first-section modal reads on the next load, and where the course component decides on load whether to celebrate; both call sites were ported by #2195 and #2196 and point at this module. When a streak is pending the first-section modal yields to it, as on `master`.
- The streak modal ([`src/shared/streak-celebration/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/tree/master/src/shared/streak-celebration)), rendered by the loaded tab page on every course tab when the course home metadata reports a streak to celebrate. Its discount line runs when the metadata also reports the streak discount enabled and the course has a verified mode: it asks the discount-code service when `DISCOUNT_CODE_INFO_URL` is set, else the ecommerce basket endpoint when `ECOMMERCE_BASE_URL` is set, else shows no discount, and renders the upgrade button with the result.
- The share buttons ([`src/courseware/social-share/`](https://github.com/openedx/frontend-app-learning/tree/master/src/courseware/social-share)): LinkedIn, X, Facebook and email, rendered inside the first-section modal, and later by the course-exit celebration, when the course has a marketing URL.
- Config reads change form per the reviewed table: `LMS_BASE_URL` and `SITE_NAME` become the site config's `lmsBaseUrl` and `siteName`; `DISCOUNT_CODE_INFO_URL`, `ECOMMERCE_BASE_URL`, `SOCIAL_UTM_MILESTONE_CAMPAIGN`, `TWITTER_URL` and `TWITTER_HASHTAG` become keys of Learning's app config under the same names, read through `getAppConfig`.

## The streak discount is a deprecation candidate

The ecommerce path calls the Oscar ecommerce stack, whose deprecation (openedx/public-engineering#22) completed on 2024-11-04 with the repositories archived; that ticket says the hooks left in other systems get their own deprecation tickets, and none has been filed for this one. The discount-code path (#1763) names no service, and neither key is set by openedx-platform or tutor-mfe, so on a stock site the line computes no discount. On the platform side the feature is the `courseware.streak_discount_enabled` course flag and `can_show_streak_discount_coupon`. This issue ports the line as it is; whether the port carries it is settled in the pull request, and a deprecation ticket, if one is filed, covers both repositories.

## Testing

The tests of the files that move here port with them: the first-section and weekly-goal modals' record-on-open behavior and the helper's section-load logic, the streak modal's open, close and discount cases against mocked discount and ecommerce requests, and the share buttons' links. They reach the page through the exported route array, courseware at a unit URL and the streak modal at any tab, with course home metadata carrying each celebration flag per the conventions #2189 set.
